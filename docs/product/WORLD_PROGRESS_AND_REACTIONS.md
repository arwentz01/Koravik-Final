# World Progress and Reaction Experience

**Status:** Approved  
**Effective build:** 024

## Visual home

World progress belongs beneath **Worlds → installed World → Progress**. Hearth may preview a recent change but never owns or edits World State.

## Required progress regions

- current arc, chapter, and scene;
- active and completed World objectives;
- narrative choices;
- NPC relationship state and explained history;
- fictional keepsakes;
- recent explainable reactions;
- permission, installation, and package status.

Every record must be visibly identified as fictional World State.

## Reaction explanation

A reaction detail must answer what changed, which approved minimized fact was received, when interpretation occurred, which World rule applied, and which private information was deliberately excluded.

## Correction rule

District truth is never rewritten by a World. Corrections use new or compensating interpretations. Historical World records remain explainable rather than being silently edited.

## Privacy boundary

World reaction screens never display Quest notes, Chronicle prose, Companion memory, account secrets, or unrelated records.
