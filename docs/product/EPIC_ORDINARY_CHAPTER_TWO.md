# Epic Ordinary — Chapter Two

**Status:** Approved
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective build:** 023

## Player outcome

Epic Ordinary has a durable World home and a second chapter, **The Eastern Room**. The player continues from the Caretaker support choice, reviews current fictional progress, understands recent changes, and chooses what kind of refuge the restored room should become.

## World home

The World home displays:

- current chapter and scene;
- one primary story action;
- the active World-owned objective;
- Caretaker relationship stage, trust, and provenance;
- the current support style;
- recent explainable World reaction;
- permission status;
- fictional keepsakes;
- a direct return to Hearth.

## Chapter Two

The Caretaker opens the eastern room without framing absence, unfinished work, or inactivity as failure. The player chooses one purpose:

- a room for rest;
- a room for making;
- a room for welcome.

The choice completes the World objective, advances the scene, creates one fictional keepsake, and records one relationship moment. It does not create, complete, or edit a real-life Quest.

## Consent and fact use

The story may display an explanation of the latest World reaction created from an approved minimized fact. Revoking Quest-completion permission stops future delivery but does not erase prior World State or historical explanation.

Epic Ordinary does not read Quest notes, Chronicle prose, Companion memory, account exports, or unrelated private records.

## Emotional safety

- Inactivity never reduces trust.
- No streak loss, guilt language, or punishment is permitted.
- Relationship changes require a plain-language reason.
- Fictional objectives remain clearly separate from real-life commitments.
- The player may leave for Hearth at any time.
