# First-Use Orientation

**Status:** Approved  
**Effective build:** 026

## Purpose

First use should explain Koravik without demanding that a person configure their life. The experience introduces ownership, consent, and the life-to-story boundary, then offers one voluntary way to begin.

## Visual homes

- Authentication owns account creation.
- First-use orientation owns the bounded welcome sequence.
- Hearth owns ongoing orientation after first use.
- Quests and Worlds own any records or actions selected from orientation.

## Required experience

A new person can:

1. create one durable Account securely;
2. understand that real-life Districts own truth;
3. understand that Worlds interpret only approved minimized facts;
4. understand that Companion suggests but does not decide;
5. choose to create a Quest, explore Epic Ordinary, or simply enter Hearth.

No World installation, Quest, Companion memory, or other consequential record is created merely by completing orientation.

## Trust boundaries

- Registration errors do not disclose whether an email already exists.
- Passwords are hashed and never stored or logged in plaintext.
- Session identifiers are regenerated after account creation.
- Orientation choices are explicit, reversible in practical effect, and never treated as personality truth.
- Existing accounts are not forced through retroactive onboarding.

## Completion rule

Orientation completion is idempotent. Hearth redirects only accounts with a pending first-use state. Source destinations remain responsible for their own validation and lifecycle.
