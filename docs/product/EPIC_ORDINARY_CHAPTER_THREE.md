# Epic Ordinary — Chapter Three

**Status:** Implemented  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective slice:** Epic Ordinary Chapter Three

## Player outcome

After restoring the Eastern Room, the player begins **The Listening Wall**. A sound behind the Library plaster asks what kind of truth the house should keep. The player interprets it; the Caretaker does not decide for them.

## Story choice

The player chooses one fictional truth:

- keep the courage it took to begin;
- keep the care that made room;
- keep what is still possible.

The choice completes one World objective, creates one fictional keepsake, advances the scene, adds one sourced story-history record, and changes Caretaker trust with a plain-language reason. Repeating either action is idempotent.

## Healing Home consequence

Healing Home may materialize the completed choice as a source-labeled Library echo and display the new keepsake there. This composition remains account-scoped and does not transfer ownership from Epic Ordinary.

The consequence does not create or change Quests, Chronicle entries, Companion memory, notifications, room notes, or real-life facts.

## Consent and safety

- Chapter Three requires the Chapter Two refuge choice.
- The Caretaker asks the player to interpret the sound rather than asserting a truth about them.
- No inactivity, streak, score, or real-world accomplishment gates the chapter.
- The player sees the exact fictional consequences before choosing.
- The story remains playable when Quest fact permission is revoked.
