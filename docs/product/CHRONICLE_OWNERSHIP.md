# Chronicle Ownership and Lifecycle

**Status:** Product contract
**Effective build:** 018

Chronicle owns saved reflections and personal memories. Entries show provenance and remain private by default.

Player-authored and player-approved entries may be edited, tagged, archived, restored, or deleted. Quest-event and World-generated historical entries are read-only in Chronicle; corrections come from their source or a compensating event.

Editing Chronicle never changes a Quest, Companion proposal, or World State. Deletion removes eligible personal content while audit evidence and source provenance follow retention policy.
