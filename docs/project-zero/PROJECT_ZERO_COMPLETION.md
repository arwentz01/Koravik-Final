# Project Zero Completion Report

**Project:** Koravik Final  
**Phase:** Product Discovery and Blueprint Foundation  
**Status:** Complete  
**Date:** July 28, 2026

## Mission

Define Koravik before implementation: its purpose, product boundaries, domain language, experience principles, architectural direction, and engineering discipline.

## Executive conclusion

Project Zero is complete because the project can now answer consistently:

- Why Koravik exists.
- What it is and is not.
- Who owns each major concept.
- How District facts and World interpretation remain separate.
- How Companion supports rather than replaces human agency.
- How the product should feel.
- How implementation decisions will be governed.

## Product interpretation

Koravik is not primarily a productivity application, habit tracker, journal, role-playing game, social network, or AI assistant. Those may exist as capabilities.

Koravik is:

> An operating system for intentional living.

Its purpose is to reduce friction between intention and action while preserving meaning.

## Established direction

Project Zero establishes:

- Hearth as the primary daily orientation surface.
- District ownership of real-life truth.
- Optional Worlds that interpret approved facts without owning or rewriting them.
- Companion as a proposing, explaining, summarizing, and drafting capability whose consequential actions require approval.
- Visible privacy and consent.
- Accessibility as a foundational requirement.
- Calm, warm, purposeful interaction design.
- Documentation-first implementation.
- Complete vertical slices rather than disconnected feature accumulation.
- Progressive simplicity as the platform grows.

## Risks reduced

Project Zero reduces the risk of:

- framework-first product design;
- hidden architectural decisions;
- module ownership ambiguity;
- World and real-life state contamination;
- AI overreach;
- engagement-driven product drift;
- dashboard bloat;
- punitive streak mechanics;
- inaccessible interaction patterns;
- undocumented implementation becoming accidental authority.

## Remaining work

## Sign-off

Project Zero is closed. The Blueprint phase is active. Production implementation remains gated until Blueprint v1.0 is reviewed, frozen, and tagged.
