# North Star

## Purpose

Koravik exists to help people live intentionally.

It should reduce friction between intention and action while preserving meaning, agency, dignity, relationships, and memory.

## Product promise

Koravik helps a person understand what matters now, take a meaningful next step, and return to life outside the application.

The best Koravik session is often a short one.

## Decision test

A capability belongs in Koravik when it does at least one of the following without compromising the principles below:

- clarifies a meaningful intention;
- makes a worthwhile action easier;
- supports health, responsibility, connection, reflection, or growth;
- preserves a meaningful experience;
- helps a person resume after interruption;
- gives an approved real-life fact meaningful narrative consequence;
- reduces avoidable administrative or cognitive burden.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Human agency comes first.
2. Districts own real-life truth.
3. Worlds interpret truth; they do not replace it.
4. Companion proposes; people approve consequential actions.
5. Privacy and consent are visible and revocable.
6. Accessibility is part of correctness.
7. Growth should make the daily experience simpler, not busier.
8. The platform must never require a Household, Organization, social graph, Companion, or World for full individual participation.
9. Returning after absence must feel welcoming, not punitive.
10. Product success is measured by value created in life, not time captured in the application.

## Success signals

Koravik should favor outcomes such as:

- meaningful intentions completed;
- reduced friction and forgotten obligations;
- stronger relationships;
- useful reflection and preserved memories;
- healthier routines without shame mechanics;
- clear continuation after interruption;
- understandable World reactions;
- increased confidence in what to do next.

Koravik must not optimize for screen time, compulsive checking, artificial streak pressure, or engagement for its own sake.
