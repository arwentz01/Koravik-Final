# Anti-Patterns

Koravik must not become any of the following.

## Engagement engine

Do not optimize for screen time, compulsive checking, infinite feeds, artificial urgency, or notification volume.

## Punitive habit tracker

Do not use shame, broken streaks, public failure, or loss mechanics to punish ordinary interruption.

## Gamified productivity skin

Narrative meaning must not reduce real life to points, badges, currency, or cosmetic rewards detached from the action itself.

## AI decision maker

Companion may suggest, explain, summarize, and draft. It must not silently make consequential decisions or bypass the owning District.

## Social network by default

Participation must not require a social graph, public profile, follower count, ranking, or performative sharing.

## Data broker or advertising platform

Personal facts, health information, relationships, private writing, and World behavior must not become inventory for advertising or opaque profiling.

## Fiction replacing reality

Worlds may interpret approved facts, but they must not overwrite District records, conceal source truth, or become required for practical tools.

## Universal dashboard

Hearth must not become a dumping ground for every module. Each addition must support orientation and preserve clear ownership.

## Hidden automation

Important reminders, suggestions, World reactions, and state changes must be explainable and traceable to their origin.

## Framework-defined product

Technical convenience must not redefine product language, ownership, user journeys, or platform principles.

## Premature distributed system

Do not introduce services, queues, event infrastructure, or operational complexity beyond what the modular monolith and shared-hosting constraints justify.

## Accessibility retrofit

Do not postpone keyboard behavior, semantics, contrast, reduced motion, readable language, or responsive layout until after feature completion.

## World-state leakage

NPC relationships, story flags, inventory, narrative progress, and World-specific achievements must not transfer to unrelated Worlds unless a separate portable contract explicitly permits it.

## Documentation theater

Documents must govern decisions and remain aligned with implementation. They must not exist merely to create the appearance of rigor.
