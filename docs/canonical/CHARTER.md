# Koravik Charter

**Version:** 3.0
**Status:** Canonical
**Rewritten:** Build 204E

---

## 1. Purpose

Koravik is a self-improvement life platform designed to help people understand themselves, care for their lives, build meaningful relationships, pursue goals, create, contribute, and experience everyday life with greater intention.

Koravik exists to support life outside the application.

It is not a game pretending to be life, a social network optimized for attention, or a productivity system that treats people as machines.

Koravik is a platform where ordinary life can feel meaningful, connected, and occasionally epic.

## 2. Product Vision

Koravik brings together the parts of life that are usually fragmented across many applications:

- daily orientation;
- health and wellness;
- goals, tasks, habits, and projects;
- events and shared experiences;
- digital identity and outreach;
- memories and reflection;
- households and organizations;
- creativity and interactive storytelling;
- supportive intelligence;
- optional play and adventure.

The Platform owns truth.

Districts own real-life records.

Worlds interpret approved truth.

Companion helps users understand and choose.

Chronicle remembers.

Quests turn intention into progress.

Hearth brings it together each day.

## 3. Who Koravik Serves

Koravik is designed for people who want practical support in living more intentionally.

It may also support:

- households;
- families;
- friends;
- nonprofits;
- clubs;
- community groups;
- volunteer organizations;
- creators;
- storytellers;
- educators;
- teams.

Membership in a Household or Organization is optional.

A person must remain able to participate fully as an individual.

## 4. Product Commitments

Koravik commits to:

- human agency;
- privacy by default;
- explicit consent;
- durable personal ownership;
- clear source-of-truth boundaries;
- modular expansion;
- accessible design;
- explainable behavior;
- reversible actions where practical;
- portability;
- safety;
- sustainable engineering;
- usefulness before novelty.

## 5. Core Product Model

Koravik is organized into:

### Platform

The shared foundation for identity, authentication, authorization, consent, settings, notifications, audit history, event delivery, search, media, installation, and other cross-cutting services.

### Districts

Durable product domains organized around human needs.

Canonical Districts include:

- Hearth
- Chronicle
- Beacon
- Gather
- Health
- Quests
- Companion
- Creator Studio
- Marketplace
- Arena

### Pillars

Enduring areas of human life Koravik supports:

- Well-being
- Growth
- Connection
- Home
- Stability
- Purpose
- Creativity
- Adventure

### Worlds

Installable interpretation and storytelling layers that may define theme, story, NPCs, dialogue, relationships, Quests, encounters, and reactions to approved Platform Events.

### Groups

Optional structures including Households and Organizations.

## 6. District Principle

Every capability must have one primary owner.

Other Districts may surface, reference, summarize, or interpret that capability, but they do not become co-owners of the same truth.

## 7. World Principle

Switching Worlds must never erase or replace the user's Platform or District data.

Each World maintains independent state.

Worlds may interpret life events but may not rewrite them.

## 8. Companion Principle

Companion proposes.

The user decides.

Consequential actions require explicit approval.

## 9. Health Principle

Koravik Health should support whole-person improvement without shame, dangerous incentives, or false clinical authority.

Health is not a substitute for licensed medical care.

## 10. Social Principle

Connection should be encouraged without coercion.

Public sharing, ranking, comparison, and participation must remain optional.

## 11. Creation Principle

Creators build content and Worlds, not Platform authority.

Creator access must remain constrained by documented schemas, permissions, event contracts, and governance.

## 12. Expansion Principle

Koravik must remain neutral enough to support future Worlds, communities, creators, Pillars, and District capabilities without rebuilding the foundation for each new idea.

## 13. Technical Direction

Koravik v3 is a custom PHP 8.3+ application.

Laravel is retired and must not be reintroduced.

The preferred architectural direction is a secure modular monolith with:

- explicit domain boundaries;
- service-oriented internal interfaces;
- versioned Platform Events;
- clear database ownership;
- migration discipline;
- testable modules;
- shared infrastructure;
- shared-hosting compatibility;
- a path to future extraction only when justified.

## 14. Success

Koravik succeeds when it helps a person:

- notice what matters;
- understand their patterns;
- take a manageable next step;
- care for their health;
- strengthen relationships;
- preserve memories;
- contribute to others;
- create something meaningful;
- recover after interruption;
- experience life with more intention.

Success is not measured only by time spent inside Koravik.
