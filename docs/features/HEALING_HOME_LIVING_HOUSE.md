# Healing Home Living House

Status: implemented vertical product slice.

This slice makes Healing Home feel more inhabited by adding invitation, threshold, and atlas surfaces around the rooms that already exist.

## Completed workflow

An authenticated person can open `/home`, choose House Invitations, Thresholds, or House Atlas, and then move into rooms through gentle, source-aware doorways.

## Product behavior

- House Invitations lists meaningful room invitations without assigning tasks.
- Thresholds groups open and waiting doorways with explicit door-state copy.
- House Atlas explains the house by meaning, story, care, making, and return.
- Room detail pages include a Room Invitation panel in addition to Room Practice.

## Boundaries

Living-house surfaces are navigation and explanation. They do not create Quests, Chronicle entries, Companion memory, Health records, Beacon attendance, Gather communication, notifications, diagnoses, score state, or hidden automation.

## Verification

Covered by the release suite test `Healing Home room expansion supports making, welcome, meaning, tending, and privacy`, extended with living-house contracts.
