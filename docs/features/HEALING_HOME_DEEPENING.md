# Healing Home Deepening

Status: implemented vertical product slice.

This slice adds authored interpretive surfaces that make Healing Home feel more inhabited without adding hidden scoring, inference, or new source ownership.

## Completed workflow

An authenticated person can open `/home`, choose Room Lore, House Constellations, Boundary Ledger, or Wayfinding, then move into rooms with clearer understanding of what each surface means and what it may do.

## Product behavior

- Room Lore gives each known room a short identity statement.
- House Constellations groups rooms into meaning, making, care, and welcome.
- Boundary Ledger explains may-show, may-suggest, must-ask-first, and must-not-touch boundaries.
- Wayfinding maps common intentions to safe room paths.

## Boundaries

These surfaces are read-only and explanatory. They do not create Quests, Chronicle entries, Companion memory, Health records, Beacon attendance, Gather communication, notifications, diagnoses, score state, hidden automation, or cross-account access.

## Verification

Covered by the release suite test `Healing Home room expansion supports making, welcome, meaning, tending, and privacy`, extended with deepening contracts.
