# Worlds and Epic Ordinary Polish

**Status:** Implemented vertical slice

This slice deepens Worlds Home and Epic Ordinary Chapter Two as one narrative-facing workflow.

## Completed workflow

- `/worlds` now opens with a story doorway for the active World.
- The active World card shows current chapter, scene, objective, latest signal, received-fact summary, relationship state, and trust as fictional World State.
- Recent reaction cards now preview approved fact and World rule before the full explanation page.
- The Worlds trust strip explains source ownership and privacy boundaries in plain language.
- `/worlds/epic-ordinary/play` now presents the Eastern Room continuation with stronger visual intrigue, explicit consent language, clear refuge choices, and post-choice links to the Eastern Room, World State, and Hearth.

## Ownership boundaries

- Worlds own fictional progress, choices, objectives, keepsakes, relationship state, and explainable reactions.
- Worlds do not own Quest completion, Chronicle prose, Companion memory, account secrets, or unrelated records.
- Epic Ordinary Chapter Two choices change fictional World State only and do not create, complete, or edit Quests.

## Accessibility and responsive behavior

The slice uses labelled sections, visible text states, keyboard-accessible links and buttons, responsive grids, and forced-color CSS support in `world-home.css` and `world.css`.
