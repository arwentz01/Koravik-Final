# Welcome-Back Experience

**Status:** Implemented vertical slice

After an extended absence, `/return` provides a calm re-entry rather than a backlog. It composes one optional story continuation, securely preserved form drafts, unread notifications, current Quest occurrences, and older intentions that may be resumed, skipped, dismissed, or rescheduled.

The page leads with a direct route to Hearth, labels all review as optional, and uses neutral language. Return composition does not take ownership of World State, drafts, notifications, or Quests. The minimized `Platform.PlayerReturned` event remains independent from the page composition and respects World fact permissions.
