# Healing Home Intrigue

Status: implemented vertical product slice.

This slice deepens Healing Home as the emotional and visual center of Koravik while preserving the architecture rule that source modules keep ownership of their records.

## Completed workflow

An authenticated person can enter `/home`, read richer door states and the house pulse, enter deeper room pages, tend the Garden, use the Workshop as an unfinished-idea shelf through private room notes, review Library explanation shelves, inspect Guest Room consent boundaries, read Eastern Room purpose copy, and open a source thread for room memories.

## Product behavior

- Door states now say what is visibly open or waiting without inventing pressure.
- House Pulse names the home’s atmosphere as presentation only, not diagnosis or scoring.
- Fireplace acts as the narrative center for explainable World echoes.
- Library groups explanation shelves for reactions, room changes, and privacy.
- Garden growth states reflect kinds of care without points or streaks.
- Workshop treats room notes as a local unfinished-ideas shelf until the person chooses Chronicle.
- Guest Room previews future consent boundaries without implementing sharing.
- Eastern Room deepens copy from the chosen Epic Ordinary purpose.
- Source threads explain source owner, source key, affected room, creation time, and privacy exclusions.

## Boundaries

This slice does not create new Quest records, Chronicle entries, Companion memory, Health records, Beacon attendance, Gather communication, notifications, hidden emotional assessments, or gamified score state.

## Verification

Covered by the release suite test `Healing Home room expansion supports making, welcome, meaning, tending, and privacy`, extended with intrigue contracts.
