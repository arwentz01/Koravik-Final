# Healing Home Room Expansion

Status: implemented vertical product slice.

This slice expands the Healing Home from a navigable foundation into a more expressive house.

## Completed workflow

An authenticated person can open `/home`, see newly meaningful rooms, enter those rooms, tend the Garden, review the house timeline, and inspect the privacy/explainability boundary entirely through the interface.

## Product behavior

- Workshop opens from an Epic Ordinary Eastern Room choice about making.
- Guest Room opens from an Epic Ordinary Eastern Room choice about welcome.
- Library opens after the account has reviewed an owned World reaction.
- Garden tending records a minimized Healing Home change and shifts the atmosphere.
- The room timeline composes Healing Home changes, keepsakes, and Caretaker conversations without becoming a scorecard.
- Room notes use the first line as a private intention label.
- The privacy panel names composed sources and explicitly names excluded sources.

## Boundaries

Healing Home composes account-owned state. It does not create Quest records, Chronicle entries, Companion memory, Health records, Beacon attendance, Gather communication, notifications, or records for other accounts.

## Verification

Covered by the release suite slice test `Healing Home room expansion supports making, welcome, meaning, tending, and privacy`.
