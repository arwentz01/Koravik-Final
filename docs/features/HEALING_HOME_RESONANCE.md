# Healing Home Resonance

Status: implemented vertical product slice.

This slice helps the Healing Home feel easier to move through once it has visual intrigue. It gives people meaningful paths without converting the house into a task list.

## Completed workflow

An authenticated person can enter `/home`, choose a House Resonance path, open the house guide, enter a room, read a room-specific practice, and follow source-thread actions back to the affected room or privacy boundary.

## Product behavior

- House Resonance offers three non-prescriptive routes: understand what changed, make without proving, and recover gently.
- Room Practice panels appear on room detail pages and suggest a context-appropriate use of the room.
- The Healing Home Guide explains routes for meaning, making, recovery, and boundaries.
- Source threads now include follow-through actions so provenance is useful, not a dead end.

## Boundaries

These paths and practices do not create Quests, Chronicle entries, Companion memory, Health records, Beacon attendance, Gather communication, notifications, hidden emotional diagnosis, or score state. They are navigation and explanation only.

## Verification

Covered by the release suite test `Healing Home room expansion supports making, welcome, meaning, tending, and privacy`, extended with resonance contracts.
