# Builds 031–035 — Journey Arc

**Status:** Implemented on `main`  
**Date:** July 28, 2026

## Build 031 — Story Invitations

Epic Ordinary may present an invitation, but it may not create a real-life Quest without an explicit player decision. Invitations support accept, decline, and seven-day snooze. Acceptance creates a destination-owned Quest with `story` provenance and a durable source reference. Decline and snooze have no punitive effect.

## Build 032 — Healing Home Expansion

The Library and Garden become open rooms. Keepsakes can be placed in rooms through account-scoped, source-idempotent records. Workshop, Guest Room, and Eastern Room remain visible extension points. Room state remains presentation state and does not own Quest, Chronicle, or World truth.

## Build 033 — Relationship Conversations

The Caretaker supports gratitude, repair, disagreement, and quiet companionship. Conversations preserve readable account-scoped history and may reference already approved relationship memories. There are no affection meters, grind loops, inactivity penalties, or correct dialogue paths.

## Build 034 — Beacon Quest Integration

Beacon-origin activity is represented by a bounded Quest proposal contract. Event preparation, attendance, volunteering, and follow-up may be proposed. A proposal is not a Quest until accepted. Beacon retains event ownership; Quests retain commitment ownership.

## Build 035 — Gather Quest Integration

Gather-origin activity uses the same consent-first proposal contract and records Gather provenance. Cooperative invitation persistence establishes future contribution and delegation boundaries without implementing Household or Organization ownership. Users remain fully capable without membership in either domain.

## Player loop

`World or supporting domain proposes → player accepts, declines, or snoozes → Quests owns accepted commitment → real action and reflection occur → Healing Home and relationships may acknowledge approved outcomes.`

## Safety and ownership boundaries

- Fictional objectives never silently become real-life Quests.
- Declining or snoozing never reduces relationship standing or Home state.
- Beacon and Gather proposals do not duplicate source-domain ownership.
- Household and Organization implementation remains deferred.
- Cooperative records are account-scoped invitation foundations, not shared-account authorization.
- World interpretation receives only approved minimized facts.
